Lego Land Florida Resort

LEGOLAND® Florida Resort spans 150 acres, making it the second-largest LEGOLAND® park in the world. It features over 50 attractions across themed lands like LEGO® Kingdoms, LEGO® NINJAGO® World, and LEGO® City. With so much to see — including the LEGOLAND Water Park, SEA LIFE Florida and Peppa Pig Theme Park — one day often isn’t enough. Why not extend the fun with a stay at one of our on-site hotels for a truly immersive experience:

  • LEGOLAND Hotel: Just steps from the park with themed rooms and daily entertainment.

  • Pirate Island Hotel: A fully interactive, pirate-themed adventure.

  • Beach Retreat: A seasonal lakeside getaway with LEGO®-themed bungalows.

Staying longer lets you enjoy more, revisit favorite attractions, and make the most of your visit — all with exclusive guest perks and unbeatable convenience.

The countdown is on for the launch of LEGOLAND® Florida Resort’s first-ever indoor family coaster, Galacticoaster! Families will board their own customizable LEGO® spacecraft and launch into an interstellar mission packed with thrills, immersive storytelling, and cosmic LEGO fun through the LEGO® Galaxy.
